# Sound and power

## Sound

Open **Audio**.

| You want | Choose |
|---|---|
| Your own output or microphone | Claim it in the list. |
| Both players on the same speakers | Main chooses **Full mix** or **Positioned stereo mix**. |
| Out of the shared mix | **Use own output** |

## Power

Main's **Session** page. Save in both games first.

| Choice | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Turn off DosPlex** | Back to plain SteamOS. The machine stays on. |
| **Sleep** | The machine sleeps. Both sessions resume. |
| **Restart**, **Shut down** | Closes Steam on both sides, then restarts or turns off. |
| **Reset my side** | Restarts your own Steam only. |

> [!TIP]
> Cannot open the menu? Main presses <kbd>Steam</kbd> and chooses **Power**, then Restart. The machine restarts into plain SteamOS.

With two players the machine does not sleep by itself.
