# What DosPlex is

Two players, one SteamOS machine, one screen. Each player has their own Steam account, library, saves, controllers and sound. You start DosPlex from Steam's library and use a controller for everything.

## What alpha means here

- DosPlex starts only when you start it. A restart of the machine brings back plain SteamOS.
- Some things are missing or rough: see [Limits of the alpha](15-limits-of-the-alpha.md).
- DosPlex sends nothing by itself. [Tell us how it went](16-tell-us-how-it-went.md) says where to write.

## What it runs on

- A Steam Machine, or a Steam Deck docked to a TV or monitor. A Deck can also use its own screen.
- Two Steam accounts and two controllers.
- The SteamOS, Gamescope and Steam client versions in the release notes. Other versions are refused unless you say so.

More: [the user guide](../user-guide.md).
