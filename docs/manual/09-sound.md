# Sound

Each player has their own sound output and microphone. The two sides can also share speakers.

On the first run Main gets the output your system already uses, for example the TV. Guest gets none until someone chooses one.

## Your own output

Open **Audio**. Claim an output (TV/HDMI, speakers or headset) and, if you want, a microphone. Release a device before the other person claims it.

- An output claim stays across restarts until you release it. A device that is off shows as disconnected, and the sound starts when it appears.
- A microphone claim is not kept. Claim it again after a restart.
- Guest's Steam shows only **DosPlex sound** and **DosPlex microphone**. Steam's audio settings cannot change the real devices.
- A long list has **Next** and **Previous**.

## Shared speakers

Main chooses a shared output and a mix:

| Choice | What you hear |
|---|---|
| **Full mix** | Both sessions through both speakers. |
| **Positioned stereo mix** | Each screen louder from its own side. Choose **Gentle**, **Balanced** or **Strong**. |
| **Stop sharing** | Ends the mix. A player with no output of their own hears nothing. |

- A player with no output of their own joins the shared speakers by itself.
- A player with a claimed output, such as a headset, keeps it until they choose **Join shared speakers**.
- **Use own output** leaves the mix, and you stay out when Main changes it.
- Both players can hear each other's audio on shared speakers. Headsets keep it apart.

A game is silent? Check the selected output and the volume.
