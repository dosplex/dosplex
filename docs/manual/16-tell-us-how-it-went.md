# Tell us how it went

This release has no feedback form, and DosPlex sends nothing by itself. Write from a phone or a computer, in your own words.

## Where

- Discord: the channel `#alpha-testers` on the DosPlex server. The invite is discord.gg/HSBJhQPA6A.
- GitHub: open an issue at github.com/dosplex/dosplex/issues for a defect you want to follow.

Both are public, with their own accounts and privacy terms.

## What helps

- What you did and what happened: the game, the side, the layout, what you expected.
- Your version: run `sudo /opt/dosplex/bin/dosplex status` in Desktop mode. It is on the first line.
- Your machine: Steam Deck or Steam Machine, the SteamOS version, what shows the picture.
- Whether it happens once, sometimes or every time.

A phone photo of the screen is often enough.

> [!WARNING]
> Never include a password, a Steam Guard code or a sign-in QR code. Check a photo before you post it.

A security problem does not belong in a public channel. `SECURITY.md` says how to report it privately.

More: [the user guide](../user-guide.md#tell-us-how-it-went).
