# Controllers

Open **Devices**. Each controller has a small number that stays with it.

- The first controller you wake is Main's.
- While Guest is on the screen and has no controller awake, the next one is Guest's.
- On a Steam Deck the built-in controls are Main's.
- A controller never changes the layout. DosPlex needs no controller to start.
- Connected controllers are listed first. An empty slot of a Steam Controller receiver is not listed.

| You want to | Do this |
|---|---|
| Take a controller | Choose **Claim** on an Unassigned controller that is awake. |
| Give it back | Choose **Release** on a controller you hold. |
| Move one (Main) | Choose **Main**, **Guest** or **Unassigned** on its row, then **Keep assignment** or **Undo**. |
| Exchange controllers (Main) | Choose **Swap controls**. |
| Drop one you no longer use | Turn it off or unplug it. Choose **Forget** on its row and confirm. |

- A move you do not confirm within 15 seconds is undone.
- If **Swap controls** asks you to press a button on a controller, do that and choose it again.
- A connected controller cannot be forgotten: release it. Forgetting keeps Bluetooth pairings and a Steam Controller's pairing with its receiver.
- A forgotten controller comes back as a new one when you turn it on: it gets a row, a number and a player by itself.
- A game that was running when you claimed a controller may need a restart to see it.

## Mouse, keyboard and Shared

The second page of **Devices** has **Mouse**, **Keyboard** and **Touchscreen**. Each starts with Main. Only Main assigns these: choose **Guest** to hand one over.

With **Shared**, a mouse crosses the middle of the screen and clicks in the side under it. A shared keyboard types where the mouse is.

## Clicking with a Steam Controller

Hold <kbd>Steam</kbd>, then use:

| Input | What it does |
|---|---|
| Right trackpad or right stick | Moves the pointer |
| Right trackpad press, or <kbd>R2</kbd> | Left click |
| <kbd>L2</kbd> | Right click |
| <kbd>D-pad Right</kbd>, <kbd>D-pad Left</kbd>, <kbd>D-pad Down</kbd> | Enter, Escape, Tab |
| Left stick | Volume |

It works only in your own side and has not been checked on a device yet. A pop-up out of reach? Switch off Steam's "Steam Controller detected!" tour.
