# Layout

The layout decides how the two players' pictures share the screen. Open Main's menu and go to the **Session** page. The row **Shared display layout** has four buttons: **Full screen**, **Side by side**, **Top / bottom** and **Guest full screen**. The row under it says which part is Main's: **Main left** or **Main right** side by side, **Main top** or **Main bottom** in Top / bottom.

**Side by side** splits the screen vertically: one player on the left, one on the right.

**Top / bottom** splits the screen horizontally: one player on top, one on the bottom.

When the screen is split, both sessions always run. There is no separate on/off control.

## Full screen

Main can take the whole screen and decide what happens to Guest:

- **Standby:** pauses Guest's session in memory. Splitting again resumes it.
- **Off:** closes Guest's session after confirmation. Save progress first. Splitting again starts Guest fresh at Steam's own sign-in or onboarding screen.

## Guest full screen

Main can also give Guest the whole screen with **Guest full screen** in the same row. Main's session goes to Standby and Guest's picture fills the screen. Use it when you quit and Guest keeps playing or watching. Guest cannot ask for it, and Guest cannot change the layout.

You can come back at any time: open your overlay with your usual keys or controller chord, then choose **Full screen**, **Side by side** or **Top / bottom**. Your session wakes from Standby. Your overlay is drawn over Guest's picture while it is open, and your keyboard and controllers keep their owners. Choosing **Full screen** from Guest full screen puts Guest in Standby.

## Standby

Standby is experimental. It keeps using memory, does not survive power loss, and online games can disconnect. Save normally before relying on it.

## Who may change the layout

Only Main changes the layout. Guest's menu has no layout control.

A change of layout asks first when it affects the other player. **Full screen** asks what happens to Guest: **Standby** or **Off...**.
