# Privacy

Dosplex runs on your own machine. It has no account, no telemetry and no analytics. The software sends nothing
to us and collects nothing about you or your machine.

## What that means

- No usage counts, no crash reports and no logs leave your machine.
- This release has no feedback form. When you want to tell us something, you write it yourself, on Discord or
  GitHub. [Tell us how it went](docs/manual/16-tell-us-how-it-went.md) says where.
- Dosplex does not look for updates by itself.

## What does use the network

- **Steam.** Each player signs in to their own Steam account, and Steam talks to Valve as it always does. That is
  between you and Valve.
- **`dosplex update`, when you run it.** It downloads the release from GitHub. Like any download, GitHub sees the
  internet address it comes from. Nothing about you or your machine is sent with it.

The software collects nothing. The project does handle a little personal data, and this is how.

- **Who is responsible.** RoyÅke ([royake.com](https://royake.com)), the business of Roy-Åke Martinsson, who is
  self-employed. Contact: <roy@dosplex.org>.
- **Mail** to <roy@dosplex.org> or <conduct@dosplex.org> is used to answer you and to handle what you report. It
  is kept as long as the matter needs, and then deleted.
- **Contributions.** If you sign the contributor license agreement, your GitHub account name and number, the pull
  request, the comment and the date are stored in the project's repository, because they prove the license you
  gave. The sign-off of a commit carries the name and email you put in it. Both stay as long as your contribution
  is in the project.
- **Discord and GitHub** are services of their own, with their own accounts and privacy terms. What you post in a
  public channel or in an issue can be read by other people. Notes that moderators make on the Dosplex server are
  kept only as long as needed to keep the community safe.
- **Donations** go through Buy Me a Coffee, which is responsible for its own processing. We see the name and the
  message you choose to leave.
- **Your rights.** You can ask what we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted, by mail to
  <roy@dosplex.org>. You do not have to say why. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in
  Spain that is the AEPD (www.aepd.es).
